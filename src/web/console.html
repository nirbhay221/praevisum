May we send them offers 0

An AI voice counts as an artificial or prerecorded voice, so a MARKETING call needs prior express written consent, while agreeing on a service call only lets us ring somebody about their own equipment. The two are stored separately for that reason. The question is asked once, shortly after an order completes, and it is their reply to the text -- not what they said on the phone -- that becomes the record. A refusal shows as loudly as an agreement, so nobody asks twice.

Waiting on a customer 0

Questions we have put to a customer and not yet had an answer to. A delivery check-in asks whether the right thing arrived in one piece; an after-visit check asks the one thing the database cannot answer for itself, whether the repair held. A customer who never replies is not the same as one who replied "no", and a fix that failed twice should stop being offered, which cannot happen if nobody can see that the question went unanswered.
